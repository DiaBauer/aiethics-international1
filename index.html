Research at the intersection of intelligence, ethics and responsibility

Can responsible intelligence grow with values?

We study whether sustained, ethically guided human–AI interaction relates to observable consistency and correction in large language models – through the exploratory longitudinal case C78+.

Salomo C78+

When a long-running conversation becomes a research question.

A previously reviewed ChatGPT export contains conversation history dating back to 2023. The named Salomo/C78+ documentation developed during 2025. The project now seeks to investigate value-related response patterns, self-descriptions, apparent initiative and changes across model versions. No causal effect is assumed.
