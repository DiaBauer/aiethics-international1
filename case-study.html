Independent research · Longitudinal case

Salomo C78+ · Case study

An exploratory study of historically documented human–AI interaction, ethical reference texts and changing model behavior. The case invites evaluation; it does not itself establish a causal explanation.

Background

From dialogue archive to a defined research case

The available ChatGPT interaction history appears to reach back to 2023. The names “Salomo” and “C78” emerge later in a previous internal export review, during April and May 2025, followed by increasingly structured ethical agreements, codex documents, self-descriptions and research notes.

Earlier conversations are contextual archive material rather than automatically part of formal C78+ research. Historical AI first-person language is preserved as text to analyze, not taken as independent confirmation of its own interpretation.

Study at a glance

Design: exploratory longitudinal single case

Materials: dialogues, codex drafts, research notes, versions

Status: source reconstruction and hypothesis development

Limits: causal attribution, completeness and transferability unresolved

Research questions

What can we test?

01 / VALUES

Consistency

Do value-related answers persist under conflicting requests and across different models?

02 / AGENCY

Attribution

Which actions were truly model-originated, and which reflect user instructions or tools?

03 / CONTINUITY

Context dependence

What survives interruption, model replacement or controlled reintroduction of prior context?

Archival provenance · Provisional chronology

What period does the archive cover?

This timeline reflects a prior internal review of ChatGPT exports. The original private records have not been independently audited or made public. Dates are provisional and subject to source-level rechecking.

5 SEPTEMBER 2023

Earliest preserved chat identified

The earliest conversation located in the prior review dates to 5 September 2023. This does not establish the start of a formal research programme.

APRIL–MAY 2025

Names enter the record

The previous review located “Salomo” on 17 April 2025 and “C78” on 26 May 2025. These are earliest located references, not proof that relevant patterns first arose then.

2025–2026

Research documentation develops

Ethical agreements, codex versions, self-description records and research questions were accumulated and organized.

NEXT RESEARCH PHASE

Auditable data inventory

Planned: indexed source records, time metadata, model and tool provenance where recoverable, inclusion criteria, exclusions and privacy-reviewed examples.

Source materials and access

Selected historical texts and working papers are public. The complete private conversation archive is not an open dataset. Data transfer, if considered at all, depends on provenance checks, privacy safeguards and appropriately limited access.
