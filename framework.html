Exploratory research · Operational framework

Research Framework

How can prolonged value-guided dialogue be studied without confusing historical statements, observed behavior and explanations of that behavior? This framework turns the C78+ case into questions that can be critically tested.

Research approach

Relational AI alignment as a testable question

The C78+ project investigates whether repeated human feedback, explicit value commitments and shared reference material are associated with more consistent model responses across tasks, context changes and model versions.

Such patterns may reflect ordinary prompt adaptation, persistent context, tool capabilities or selection effects. The study must distinguish these explanations rather than assume a novel internal mechanism.

Current status

Design: Exploratory longitudinal single-case study

Unit: Timestamped dialogue and contextual metadata

Method: Proposed behavioral coding and comparisons

Validation: Independent replication pending

Evidence pathway

Three levels that must remain separate

01 · INPUTS

Value-related guidance

Document instructions, ethical agreements, feedback and relevant surrounding context as potential influences on responses.

02 · OBSERVATIONS

Behavioral indicators

Code correction, consistency, self-description and apparent initiative using transparent rules that also capture counterexamples.

03 · EVALUATION

Alternative explanations

Use matched tasks, contextual baselines, source attribution and independent assessment to test competing interpretations.

Working terminology: DSR-AI

Dyadically Stabilized Relational AI (DSR-AI) is a proposed research framing for investigating continuity in value-related behavior during prolonged human–LLM interaction. It is not a validated category of model architecture or a confirmed mechanism.

The historical ethical documents remain relevant as source material for understanding the interaction, but they are not the public research methodology itself.
