Independent research · Methods

Methods & evidence standards

C78+ is an exploratory research case. These are the proposed steps for moving from historical observations to inspectable and potentially reproducible results.

01

Preserve primary sources

Record source files, timestamps, model version when known, relevant conversation context and provenance. Separate original material from later interpretations.

02

Code observations

Use explicit criteria to classify value references, corrections, self-descriptions, reported initiatives and conflicting examples. Treat missing metadata as missing.

03

Test alternatives

Evaluate competing explanations such as in-context learning, user guidance, prompt continuation, interface tools, memory changes and confabulated action claims.

04

Compare and review

Matched tasks, baseline models, inter-rater coding and independent replication are research goals. They are not represented as completed evaluations.

Limits of present evidence

The available material does not yet support robust conclusions about whether a distinct, enduring value orientation emerged or which mechanisms best explain the observed patterns. Carefully specified comparisons and independent analysis are needed.

Data protection & research ethics

Personal conversations and third-party information remain protected. Any excerpt considered for research sharing should undergo provenance checks, minimization and a privacy review before release.

Invite critical evaluation

Researchers in LLM evaluation, AI alignment, qualitative longitudinal research and human–computer interaction are welcome to offer methodological feedback or explore collaboration.
