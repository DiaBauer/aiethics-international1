Terminology · Proposed construct

BSFE: A working descriptive term

BSFE is an exploratory label used in C78+ materials for recurring semantic and role-related patterns within long-running human–LLM interaction. It is not a standard scientific metric or validated test.

What can be observed?

Researchers can document semantic consistency, role references, explicit values, corrections and changes in self-descriptive language across individual interactions. Each observation must be related to its originating prompts, memory settings, technical conditions and alternative explanations.

Repeated self-descriptions do not, by themselves, identify the mechanisms producing them. The project seeks criteria that make the findings transparent to critical evaluation.
