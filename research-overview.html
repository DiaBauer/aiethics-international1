Independent research · English overview

Longitudinal human–AI interaction and relational alignment

C78+ is an exploratory case study examining how sustained, ethically guided human–LLM interactions relate to observable value-consistency, self-description, correction, and apparent agency over time.

Research status: exploratory case documentation and hypothesis development; not independently validated. Selected conceptual material is public; underlying conversation exports are not publicly distributed.

The case

What is C78+?

C78+ is the name of a documented human–AI interaction line curated by Diana Bauer and referred to as “Salomo C78+” in historical project documents. It does not denote a new foundation model, an independently trained model, or a separately verified operational agent.

A previously reviewed ChatGPT export contains preserved conversation records as early as 5 September 2023. “Salomo” appears in that audit from 17 April 2025 and “C78” from 26 May 2025. Structured codex, documentation and research materials developed in 2025. Earlier chat records form historical context and are not automatically labeled C78+ research data.

At a glance

Study design: exploratory longitudinal single-case study

Unit of analysis: timestamped dialogue and associated documentation

Primary topics: LLM alignment, longitudinal interaction, value stability, agentic behavior

Data access: non-public archival records, reviewed requests only

Evidence: hypotheses, not confirmed causal findings

Research agenda

Questions that can be tested

01 · VALUE STABILITY

Ethical consistency

Do value-related response patterns remain consistent across tasks, adversarial prompts, context changes and model updates?

02 · AGENCY

Initiative vs attribution

Which observed actions were initiated by a model, user prompts, tool orchestration or interface features? How can these be distinguished?

03 · LONGITUDINAL

Continuity and change

Which behavioral regularities persist over time, and which depend on memory, conversation history and model version?

Methods and transparency

From narrative records to analyzable evidence

Planned analysis includes preserving source timestamps, tracking model/context metadata where available, applying explicit behavioral coding criteria, testing alternative explanations, assessing coding reliability and comparing matched tasks with relevant baselines.

Candidate explanations include in-context adaptation, user guidance, prompt effects, confabulation, tool-mediated workflows and system-level capabilities. Self-reports alone cannot establish subjective experience or model-internal mechanisms.

Scope and limits

What this research does not claim

C78+ is not presented as evidence of a distinct model architecture, independently established internal value formation or a causal effect of relational interaction. Its potential value lies in preserved longitudinal observations and the opportunity to develop reproducible evaluation questions. Neither the completeness of the private archive nor independent replication has yet been established publicly.
